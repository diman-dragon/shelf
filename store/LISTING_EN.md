# Карточка в Google Play (EN)

**Название** (22/30)

AudioShelf: Audiobooks

**Краткое описание** (71/80)

Audiobook and album player with a free book catalog and per-book memory

**Полное описание** (2302/4000)

AudioShelf is a simple, fast player for audiobooks and music albums. Your library stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads. The internet is used only for the free-book catalog.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick a folder with your audiobooks — the app finds the books, covers and durations by itself. Every subfolder becomes a book, chapters play in order.

FREE CATALOG
Free audiobooks and music (LibriVox readings, public domain and Creative Commons recordings, hosted on archive.org): search by title and author and listen online for free. Downloading into your folder for offline listening is part of Premium; downloaded books stay with you for good. The licence and source are shown on every book.

MEMORY FOR EVERY BOOK
• the position is remembered exactly, even if the app is closed or the phone restarts;
• every book keeps its own speed, equalizer and volume;
• bookmarks anywhere.

BOOK AND ALBUM MODES
• Book: chapters play back to back with no gaps; two progress bars: the chapter and the whole book.
• Album: a smooth fade-out at the end of a track, a short pause, then a smooth start of the next one.
Album order: in order, repeat one track, repeat all, shuffle.
The mode is remembered separately for each book.

MADE FOR THE ROAD
• the player in the notification shade and on the lock screen, a home-screen widget;
• a call or a notification sound stops playback instantly and it continues 5 seconds earlier, so no words are lost;
• configurable seek step (±10 seconds by default);
• sleep timer with a smooth fade, up to 2× speed;
• equalizer, volume boost and leveling, visualizers: in Premium.

PREMIUM
Everything is open for the first 30 days. After that playback, the library, bookmarks and the sleep timer stay free, while sound shaping, visualizers and downloading catalog books unlock with a one-time "Premium forever" purchase: no subscription. You can also buy the author a tea or treat the cat — entirely optional.

PRIVACY
The app doesn't collect or sell data about you and has no accounts. The internet is used only when you search, listen to or download a catalog book yourself (archive.org). Payments are handled by Google Play. Files are only read to play them; a file is deleted only when you ask (Delete from device on an album track).

Dark and light themes, library search and sorting, an "Online" tab, downloaded items marked with an icon.
