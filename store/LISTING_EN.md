# Карточка в Google Play (EN)

**Название** (22/30)

AudioShelf: Audiobooks

**Краткое описание** (64/80)

Audiobook and album player: per-book memory, no internet, no ads

**Полное описание** (1291/4000)

AudioShelf is a simple, fast player for audiobooks and music albums. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads, no internet.

HOW IT WORKS
Pick a folder with your audiobooks — the app finds the books, covers and durations by itself. Every subfolder becomes a book, chapters play in order.

MEMORY FOR EVERY BOOK
• the position is remembered exactly, even if the app is closed or the phone restarts;
• every book keeps its own speed, equalizer and volume;
• bookmarks anywhere.

BOOK AND ALBUM MODES
• Book: chapters play back to back with no gaps; two progress bars: the chapter and the whole book.
• Album: a smooth fade-out at the end of a track, a short pause, then a smooth start of the next one.
The mode is remembered separately for each book.

MADE FOR THE ROAD
• the player in the notification shade and on the lock screen, a home-screen widget;
• a call or a notification sound stops playback instantly and it continues 5 seconds earlier, so no words are lost;
• configurable seek step (±10 seconds by default);
• sleep timer with a smooth fade, up to 2× speed, skip silence;
• equalizer with automatic overload protection.

PRIVACY
The app collects and transmits no data. Access to your folders is read-only.

Dark and light themes, a "For you" screen with shelves, library search and sorting.
